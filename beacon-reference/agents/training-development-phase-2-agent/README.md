# Training and Development

Training & Development is an AI-powered conversational assistant that helps employees discover, review, and manage their training activity, while also supporting training budget management and course administration. It lets employees browse the training calendar, look up full details for a specific course before applying, and view their own complete training history and status - attended, applied, registered, direct-enrolled, upcoming, training needs, and externally recorded training. It also lets administrators instantly query training budget allocations and utilization across organizational hierarchies and financial periods, and guides them through an interactive process to generate well-structured, professional course descriptions by collecting key details such as topic, audience, duration, and learning objectives.
